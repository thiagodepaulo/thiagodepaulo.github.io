## Research Projects

### Coarsening Algorithms in K-Partite Graphs
*2022–2025 · Coordinator · Department of Computer Science, UnB*

Coarsening methods for k-partite networks that reduce storage and classification time while preserving classification performance in semi-supervised models. Experiments on hundreds of thousands of synthetic networks showed over one-third savings in storage and classification twice as fast.

* Team: Thiago de Paulo Faleiros, Paulo Althoff, Alan Demétrius Baria Valejo
* Related publications: *Coarsening effects on k-partite network classification* (Applied Network Science, 2023); *Analyzing the Impact of Coarsening on k-Partite Network Classification* (BRACIS 2024 / LNCS, 2025)

### Osíris – Inteligência Artificial em Execução Fiscal
*2022–2024 · Member · Campus Gama, UnB*

Research and development of AI solutions to help the Attorney General's Office of the Federal District (PGDF) handle tax enforcement proceedings, and training of its staff to maintain and evolve the results.

* Coordinator: Fabricio Ataides Braz


### [KnEDLe – kNowledge Extraction from Documents of LEgal content](https://unb-knedle.github.io/)
*2020–2023 · Coordinator · Department of Computer Science, UnB*

Intelligent tools to extract structured information from official publications such as the Official Gazette of the Federal District (DODF), making information easier to search and retrieve, increasing government transparency, and supporting the auditing of public spending. Funded by FAPDF, UnB, and Finatec.

* Team: Thiago de Paulo Faleiros, Vinicius Ruela Pereira Borges, Marcelo Grandi Mandelli, Luis Paulo Faina Garcia, Andrei Lima Queiroz, Ricardo Marcondes Marcacini, Carol Alves Okimoto
* Students: 10 undergraduate, 4 master's, 1 Ph.D.
* Website: [unb-knedle.github.io](https://unb-knedle.github.io/)
* Video lectures: [KnEDLe playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKgKvshGQ0jFLzl6dSlMHdkiurCQM_QkS)
* Related publications: *DODFMiner: An automated tool for Named Entity Recognition from Official Gazettes* (Neurocomputing, 2024); *Legal Document Segmentation and Labeling Through Named Entity Recognition Approaches* (JIDM, 2024)

### Projeto de Reformulação da Legislação Anotada do STF
*2018 · Coordinator · Faculty of Law, UnB*

Evaluation and redesign of the Brazilian Supreme Court's (STF) *Legislação Anotada*, the publication that presents the Constitution with the relevant rulings for each provision, establishing a data model that links legal and constitutional texts to the Court's case law.

* Team: Thiago de Paulo Faleiros, Alexandre Araújo Costa

### Extração de Tópicos em Fluxo de Documentos Textuais
*2012–2016 · Ph.D. project · ICMC, University of São Paulo · FAPESP*

Text mining techniques for extracting topics from continuous streams of documents (news, web logs), detecting the origin and evolution of topics over time with methods based on complex networks.

* Advisor: Alneu de Andrade Lopes

### Avaliação do Modelo Sintático de Tópico (FAPESP BEPE)
*2013–2014 · Research internship abroad · UMIACS, University of Maryland · FAPESP*

Evaluation methodologies for the Syntactic Topic Model (STM), a Bayesian nonparametric model of parsed documents that combines topic models with syntactic parse trees.

* Team: Alneu de Andrade Lopes (coordinator), Jordan Boyd-Graber

### Problemas de Classificação e Particionamento
*2009–2010 · M.S. project · Institute of Computing, Unicamp · FAPESP*

Efficient algorithms (randomized, approximation, and heuristic) for NP-hard classification and partitioning problems on similarity graphs.

* Coordinator: Eduardo Cândido Xavier

### LinkDigger-*
*2008 · Research and development · Innolution – Sistemas de Informática · FAPESP and CNPq*

A system for the automatic creation of links between textual documents.

* Coordinator: José Antônio Camacho Guerreiro
